The builder

Three panes.
Zero guesswork.

  1. 1Drag fields in

    Field types on the left, your form in the middle. Drag to add, drag to reorder — or use the buttons and keyboard instead.

  2. 2Tune every detail

    Select a field to change its label, make it required, or add conditional logic in the inspector on the right.

  3. 3Preview the real thing

    Try the form exactly as visitors will see it. Nothing you enter in the preview is sent or saved.

  4. 4Publish & share

    Changes save as a draft by themselves. Hit Publish, then add it with the block, a shortcode, or an embed snippet.

Where VuloForm stands out

  • ✓ Every feature is in the free plugin — there is no paid tier to upsell you to.
  • ✓ Conditional logic and calculations are enforced again on your server, not just in the browser.
  • ✓ Webhooks are signed, retried twice and logged on each submission — free.
  • ✓ Spam protection needs no CAPTCHA and sends no visitor data to another company.
  • ✓ No account, no telemetry, and IP addresses aren't stored unless you switch it on.

Where others are stronger

  • • Payments. VuloForm doesn't take payments. WPForms, Formidable, Fluent Forms and Forminator include Stripe for free.
  • • Integrations. The paid tiers of WPForms, Gravity Forms and others connect directly to CRMs, email-marketing tools and Zapier. VuloForm offers webhooks.
  • • Track record. VuloForm is at version 1.0. The others have years of releases and large communities behind them.
  • • Specialist extras. Things like quizzes, e-signatures, PDF generation and user registration are available elsewhere.

Publish anywhere

One form.
Every place you need it.

Drop it on a WordPress page, or paste a tiny snippet into a site that doesn't run WordPress at all. Submissions always land back home. 

<!-- wp:vuloform/form {"formId":12} /-->

Add the VuloForm block to any page or post and pick a published form from the list. 

[vuloform id="12"]

Paste it into a page, a text widget, or any page builder's shortcode element. 

<?php echo vuloform_render( 12 ); ?>

Render a form straight from your theme or plugin. 

<div data-vuloform="12"></div>
<script src="https://example.com/wp-content/plugins/vuloform/
  assets/js/public/vuloform-embed.min.js
  ?site=https%3A%2F%2Fexample.com%2Fwp-json" async></script>

Landing page, shop or static site — not an iframe, so it inherits the host page's fonts and width.

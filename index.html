FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I create a contact form in WordPress with VuloForm?

Go to VuloForm › New form, choose the Contact template, adjust the fields, publish, and add the form to a page with the VuloForm block or shortcode.

Is VuloForm really drag and drop?

Yes. Drag field types from the library onto your form, drag to reorder, and edit settings on the right. You can also click a field type to add it to the end.

Can I build multi-step forms?

Yes. Add a Page break field wherever a new step should start. Visitors see Next and Back buttons and a step indicator.

Can I make a quote or price calculator?

Yes. Combine number fields with a Calculation field. The result is shown live and recalculated on the server when the form is sent.

How do I stop spam without CAPTCHA?

Every form includes a hidden trap field, a minimum-time check and a rate limit. Add Google reCAPTCHA v2 or v3 per form if you need more.

Does VuloForm make my site GDPR compliant?

VuloForm gives you tools such as consent fields, retention, export and erasure. It does not make a site compliant by itself; how you use the tools is up to you.
